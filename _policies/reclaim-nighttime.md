# The Nighttime Recovery Standard

## 1. The Core Mandate
All residential units must be capable of reaching a **Safe Recovery Temperature** of <span data-measure="temp" data-value="26">26°C</span> for at least 8 continuous hours between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM.

## 2. The Science (Why?)
Tropical nights prevent cardiovascular reset.
> "Mortality risk increases by ~10-60% during tropical nights."
> — [WHO Heat Guidelines](evidence/who-heat-health.txt)

## 3. Implementation
### 3.1 Passive First
Before mechanical cooling is installed, landlords must install **external shading** on South/West facing windows.

### 3.2 The "Cool Room" Variance
If central AC is not feasible, the landlord may designate one "Recovery Room" that meets the standard.

## 4. Compliance Timeline

1.  **12-Month Retrofit Clock:** Within 12 months of ordinance adoption, every landlord shall file a compliance plan identifying which units already meet the <span data-measure="temp" data-value="26">26°C</span> recovery target and which require upgrades.
2.  **Seasonal Readiness Date:** By 1 April of each year, property owners shall certify that passive shading, ventilation strategies, and any mechanical systems are operational before the first forecasted heat wave.
3.  **Emergency Activation:** During officially declared heat emergencies, landlords must switch to the "Cool Room" schedule within 24 hours and notify tenants of the designated space.

## 5. Enforcement & Verification

1.  **Rental License Conditioning:** No rental license, occupancy permit, or condominium registration shall be issued or renewed unless the owner submits the annual Nighttime Recovery Certification (temperature log plus shading inventory).
2.  **In-Unit Sensors:** Housing inspectors shall deploy calibrated data loggers during random audits and whenever a tenant files a heat complaint. A single failed test triggers a correction order with a 14-day retrofit deadline.
3.  **Penalties & Tenant Protections:** Owners that miss the correction deadline shall pay fines of €500 per dwelling per day and must provide temporary cooling or hotel accommodation at their expense. Third offenses escalate to license suspension and public disclosure on the municipal registry.

## 6. Metrics & Transparency

- **Compliance Rate:** Publish the percentage of certified units and highlight wards falling below 90% compliance.
- **Health Outcomes:** Track emergency room visits for nighttime heat stress and correlate with compliance data.
- **Upgrade Pipeline:** Report how many units received passive shading versus mechanical cooling so future subsidies can be targeted.
