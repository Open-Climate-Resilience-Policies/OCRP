# External Resources

A curated list of external guides, frameworks, and databases that complement the OCRP policy library. These resources help advocates, drafters, and implementers write stronger, more enforceable climate and circular-economy policies.

---

## Policy Drafting & Implementation Guides

### The Policy Playbook
*The Mandarin* — a practical field guide for public servants and advocates who need to turn ideas into effective legislation.

- **What it covers:** Policy framing, stakeholder engagement, drafting principles, implementation roadmaps, and evaluation frameworks.
- **Why it matters:** Translates theory into actionable steps that can strengthen the mandates and enforcement mechanisms in any OCRP policy.
- **Access:** [Download PDF](https://www.themandarin.com.au/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2026/01/The-Policy-Playbook_ebook.pdf) *(The Mandarin, January 2026)*

---

## Circular Economy Legal Instruments

### CircuLaw
*Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences & partners* — an open database of legal instruments available to governments to accelerate the circular economy transition.

- **What it covers:** EU directives, national legislation (Netherlands-focused but broadly applicable), and municipal instruments across themes such as construction, food, plastics, and textiles.
- **Why it matters:** Provides ready-made legislative precedents and legal hooks for policies in OCRP categories including [Circular Construction]({{ '/policies/circular-construction-model/' | relative_url }}), [Green Procurement]({{ '/policies/green-procurement/' | relative_url }}), [E-Waste]({{ '/policies/e-waste/' | relative_url }}), and [Food & Organic Waste]({{ '/policies/food-and-organic-waste/' | relative_url }}).
- **Access:** [circulaw.nl](https://www.circulaw.nl/) *(last verified: 2026-05-03)*
